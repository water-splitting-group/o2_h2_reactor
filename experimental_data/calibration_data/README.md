Contains experimental data from calibration of oxygen and hydrogen sensor.

`GC_Unisense`contains original and processed data from gas phase H2 measurements with gas chromatography and Unisense sensor.

`H2_gas_phase`contains original and processed data from calibration of Unisense sensor in gas phase.

`H2_liquid_phase`contains original and processed data from calibration of Unisense sensor in liquid phase.

`O2_calibration`contains original data from calibration of Pyroscience sensor.
